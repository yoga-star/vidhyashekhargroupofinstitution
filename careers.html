We're Hiring

Current Opening

We're expanding our admissions team and looking for confident communicators who can guide students and parents with clarity and care.

Admission Counsellor

Full-time Nelamangala, Bengaluru 2–3 yrs experience

What we're looking for

  • Experienced staff with strong, confident communication skills
  • Fluency in Kannada, Hindi and English
  • 2–3 years of previous experience in the same field (education / admissions counselling) preferred
  • Comfortable speaking with students and parents in person, on phone and on WhatsApp
  • Warm, patient and target-oriented attitude

Key responsibilities

  • Counsel students & parents on courses, eligibility, fees and the admission process
  • Respond to enquiries and follow up on leads promptly
  • Support the admissions team through the counselling-to-enrolment journey

How to apply

Send your resume by email or WhatsApp with the subject “Admission Counsellor Application”. Shortlisted candidates will be called for an interview.
