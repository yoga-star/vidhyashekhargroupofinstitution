Nursing College in Nelamangala, Bengaluru

Vidhya Shekhar College of Nursing — B.Sc Nursing, GNM & M.Sc Nursing affiliated to RGUHS, with real bedside training at our own Northwest Hospital on campus.

RGUHS Affiliated

Train Where the Patients Are

For students across Nelamangala, Dabaspet, Tumakuru and north-west Bengaluru who dream of a nursing career, Vidhya Shekhar College of Nursing offers something rare: a recognised RGUHS-affiliated nursing programme with a multi-speciality teaching hospital right on campus. You won't travel to distant hospitals for practicals — you'll train at Northwest Hospital from emergency triage to post-operative recovery, under expert supervision.

Nursing remains one of the most secure and globally portable careers in healthcare. Our structured progression — from GNM diploma to B.Sc and M.Sc — lets you enter at the level that fits your eligibility and grow into senior clinical, teaching or administrative roles.
