Paramedical College in Nelamangala — Join After 10th / SSLC

Vidhya Shekhar College of Paramedical Science — PMB-Karnataka recognised diplomas in Lab, Imaging, OT & Anaesthesia and Dialysis Technology, with hands-on training at our own Northwest Hospital.

Paramedical Board of Karnataka

A Healthcare Career Straight After 10th

You don't need a science PUC to begin a healthcare career. Our College of Paramedical Science in Nelamangala opens the door for 10th / SSLC pass students to enter the medical field through recognised diploma training — a practical, affordable path that leads to stable, in-demand jobs.

Affiliated to the Paramedical Board of Karnataka, each diploma runs for 3 years plus a 6-month clinical internship. The curriculum blends core medical theory with intensive hands-on practice, so you graduate able to operate lab and imaging equipment, assist in operation theatres, and manage dialysis procedures with confidence — training right at our on-campus hospital instead of travelling elsewhere.

Where It Leads

Jobs After a Paramedical Diploma

Lab Technician (DMLT)

Work in hospital labs, diagnostic chains and pathology centres handling blood, samples and reports.

Imaging / X-Ray Technician (DMIT)

Operate X-ray, CT and ultrasound equipment in radiology and diagnostic departments.

OT & Anaesthesia Technician

Assist surgical teams and anaesthetists in operation theatres across multi-speciality hospitals.

Dialysis Technician (DDT)

Manage dialysis machines and patient care in nephrology units and standalone dialysis centres.

On-Campus Hospital Training

Real clinical hours at Northwest Hospital — you practise on actual equipment, not just in classrooms.

Pathway to Degrees

A diploma can be a stepping stone to a B.Sc in Allied Health Sciences later, broadening your options.
