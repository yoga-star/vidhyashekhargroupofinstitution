Law College in Nelamangala, Bengaluru

Vidhya Shekhar College of Law — a 3-year LLB built on practice, not just theory: moot courts, legal aid clinics, court visits, internships and dedicated judicial-services preparation.

Legal Education

Train to Argue, Advise & Lead

The legal profession is built on precision, persuasion and principle. Our College of Law in Nelamangala offers a 3-year LLB programme for graduates who want to enter the legal field — whether your ambition is to argue cases in court, advise corporations, draft policy, or serve in the judiciary.

The curriculum covers every major branch of Indian law: Constitutional, Criminal, Contract, Property, Corporate, Environmental and Intellectual Property law. But we go beyond the textbook — students take part in moot court competitions, legal aid clinics, court visits and internships with practising advocates and law firms, developing the advocacy and research skills that separate competent lawyers from exceptional ones.

For students drawn to public service, our faculty provides focused preparation for judicial-services examinations and the civil-services law optional. For those headed to the corporate world, the programme builds a strong foundation in commercial and regulatory law — opening doors to corporate counsel, compliance and legal-consultancy roles.
